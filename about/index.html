ABOUT THE PROJECT

Meet Abul AI.

Abul AI is a conversational AI project built around a straightforward idea: make it easy to start a conversation in Bangla or English.

A conversational project

Abul brings a chat interface and language options together in one focused experience. You can start a new chat, choose your preferred language and ask your question.

Created by Talha Muttakin

Abul AI is created by Talha Muttakin, a creative developer who works across frontend and backend development.

Start a conversation

Visit the chat to try Abul AI. Choose বাংলা, English, or the combined language option and begin with your own question.
