Prefer English? Choose English from the language selector and start a conversation. You can use the chat to explore ideas, ask everyday questions and work through topics step by step.

What is a Bangla AI assistant?

A Bangla AI assistant is a conversational tool designed to respond to prompts written in Bengali. Abul AI provides a chat interface with Bangla and English language choices, bringing both options into one place.
